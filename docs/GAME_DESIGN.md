# Game Design

## Adventure milestone — 2026-10-05

The playable loop is Town → Training Yard/Forest/Lake/Ruins → combat or gathering → resource buyers and introductory quests. A new account receives a sword, three healing potions, fishing rod and axe without a wallet. Town separates social space, western combat vendors, eastern cosmetics, northeastern Trading Hall and southern resource buyers. No PvP. Parties of up to four have a schema foundation only; cooperative parties and bosses are not a delivered mode.
