# Mochi World product vision

Premium painterly isometric browser social/adventure game. Human villagers explore a purposeful Town/social plaza, Trading Hall/markets, Training Yard, Whispering Forest, Moonwater Lake and Old Ruins with distinctive beast companions. PixiJS is primary; DOM overlays provide readable complex menus. Three prototypes are historical.

The core loop is explore → fight/gather → resources and controlled rewards → buy/equip/upgrade → harder content and persistent Mochi experiences → socialize/trade. Fishing and woodcutting are required. Basic gameplay needs neither a wallet nor bought tokens. Four weapon families, five spells, equipment, useful consumables and coherent progression belong to release.

Cadence owns companion intent and paper-trading decisions in separate persistent domains. Deterministic code owns physical execution and game authority. No hidden policy replaces an invalid brain choice. Battle usefulness must be measured or the player promise honestly adjusted; current evidence supports NO PROMOTION. Public phrases/emotes and private owner conversation have distinct privacy rules.

Production economy uses configured SPL $MOCHI with integer accounting and controlled backed claims; test currency is labeled. No autonomous real-money operations, hot treasury signing keys, public launch or production-data destruction.

Release is a finite cohesive game, governed by PRODUCT_DONE.md. Guilds, auctions, free-form public messaging, additional gathering professions and 3D prototype work are post-release unless an audited core dependency requires them. Audio feedback/settings are required; music/voice acting are post-release. Experimental battle training never changes a published pack in place.
