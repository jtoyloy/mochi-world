# Trading engine

Trading stays autonomous PAPER trading. The existing six trade motors, allocation/drawdown/
hourly/cooldown/sleep gates, price normalization/provider masks, portfolio math, native Cadence
host and competition isolation/settlement remain. No real $MOCHI enters a simulated portfolio.

Exchange props open the existing Lab/portfolio UI. Arena props open actual cups and leaderboard.
Mochi dialogue can narrate its own saved decision/actual paper summary; text does not execute
orders or claim hidden neural reasoning. Public tick intervals remain five minutes normal and
one minute in cups; rendering/network clocks never schedule financial decisions.

MARKET_MODE=mock and MOCK_MARKET_DATA=true use labeled deterministic data. Live CoinGecko
remains optional; missing history stays unavailable and fallback is labeled. Public competitions
still require production identity/checkpoint provenance before claiming fair paid competition.
Existing rewards remain test/game points, not automatic token payouts or investment returns.
